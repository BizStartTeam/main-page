How it works

Three simple steps. Big buttons, pictures and voice help.

  1. 1

    Install the app

    Get BizStart from Google Play, or open it in your phone's browser and add it to the home screen.

  2. 2

    Choose your path

    I have a business — write down every sale and every cost.
    I have an idea — list what you need and what it costs.

  3. 3

    See your money clearly

    BizStart adds it up in KES: what is left today, this week, this month — and how much you still need to start.

📶

Works offline

Saves on your phone at the market. Syncs when you are back online.

🔊

Listen, don't read

Tap the speaker to hear any screen read out loud.

📱

Light and fast

Small download, made for older phones and slow 3G.
